# Official store badges

Downloaded unchanged 2026-10-25; do not redraw, recolor, crop or distort.

- `app-store.svg`: https://developer.apple.com/assets/elements/badges/download-on-the-app-store.svg
- `google-play.png`: https://play.google.com/intl/en_us/badges/static/images/badges/en_badge_web_generic.png

Usage: [Apple guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/marketing/guidelines/),
[Google guidelines](https://partnermarketinghub.withgoogle.com/brands/google-play/google-play/lockups-icons-badges/).
Apple artwork displays at 40 px high; Google PNG includes clear space and displays at 60 px high.
Components provide additional space around badges. Mobile shows its device's store only; desktop Apple first.
Promotion stays disabled until both apps are available and verified listing URLs are configured.
