Subject: Hightop Challenge TV: Monetization policy issue fixed and resubmitted

Hello Amazon Developer Support,

Thank you for explaining why Hightop Challenge TV (App ID amzn1.devportal.mobileapp.40a765b23bb84ce49109cafe420be156)
was rejected. We have fixed the issue and resubmitted the app.

What we changed:

- The app is now consumption only. The QR code on the Connect screen opens a sign-in page for existing Hightop
  Challenge partner accounts. That page no longer links to creating an account, pricing or any purchase. A signed-in
  user without a venue now sees a message instead of a sign-up page.
- No account can be created, and nothing can be bought, in the app or through any QR code it shows.
- The app description and testing notes now say that the app is for venues that already have a Hightop Challenge
  partner account.

For context: the app has no in-app purchases and sells no digital content. It shows a venue's trivia game on the
venue's TV, and guests play free on their phones. The other QR code on the display screens ("Get the app") is for
guests' phones. It opens our free player app, which has no purchases.

You can review the app without an account. Choose "Try demo" on the Connect screen, or type the display code J6NK to
open our test venue.

Please let us know if you need anything else.

Best regards,
