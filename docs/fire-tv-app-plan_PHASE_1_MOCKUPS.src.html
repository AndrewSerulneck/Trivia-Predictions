A · TV app

Connect screen

The partner picks one of two ways. Option 1 is a phone scan. Option 2 is typing the 4-character display code with the remote. The gold OR shows they don't need both.

I used "Option 1 / Option 2" instead of "Method #1 / #2" because it reads more naturally. Say if you prefer "Method". This screen has no "Get the app" QR: a second QR here would confuse the person setting up the TV. When the code expires the TV shows "Press OK for a new code".

  • What takes over the TV, in order: a Live Trivia game, then the pre-game hour before it, then a Category Blitz round people are playing, then Super Bowl Squares or the March Madness Bracket, then this carousel.
  • When a game ends, the carousel starts again at slide 1.
  • A slide with nothing to show is skipped. No Live Trivia scheduled means no slide 1. No games today means no slide 2. The loop gets shorter, never empty.
  • One request feeds every slide. Rotating costs nothing extra.

exists The TV already gets the schedule's weekly days, so "Every Thursday" needs no new data. Today it says "Live Trivia · Thursday 8:00 PM" even when the game is 9 days away. The date rule above fixes that. C3 is today's pre-game screen with a big "LIVE TRIVIA" added, starting 60 minutes early instead of 15. A Category Blitz round people are already playing stays on screen until 15 minutes before start, so we don't cut off a round in progress.

D · Carousel slide 2

Pick 'Em Live Board

The 4 games that matter most at this bar right now, how this bar picked them, and the top 5. The ticker scrolls the other games and never repeats a game that's on the board.

  • How the TV picks the games. Every game today gets a score from simple rules, and the top 4 go on the board:
    • The bar's own picks. Games more people here picked rank higher. This is the smartest signal: it learns what this crowd cares about, for free.
    • Day and time. NFL leads on Sunday afternoons and Monday and Thursday nights. College football leads on Saturdays. NBA and NHL lead on weeknights. Soccer leads on weekend mornings.
    • Live now beats starting soon, which beats finished. Finals drop off after an hour.
    • Big games (playoffs, national primetime) get a boost when the sports feed marks them.
  • Seasons take care of themselves. A league only counts when it has games today, so there's no calendar to keep up.
  • not yet College basketball (men's and women's) isn't in Pick 'Em today. Adding it is a separate small project, priced first. Once it's added, the TV ranks it like any other league.
  • data TBD Live scores and the game clock aren't stored yet (see the plan's Phase 9). exists Pick split and top 5. Missing data shows "–", never 0.

E · Carousel slide 3 · new

All-Time Leaderboard

The bar's top 10 players of all time, across every game. Gold is the one accent, for #1.

exists These are the same all-time points the players' leaderboard shows. It's one small read of 10 rows, cached for 15 seconds, so 100 TVs cost about the same as one. If a bar has fewer than 3 players, the slide is skipped.

Ticker and "Get the app" QR

The ticker runs only on carousel slides. The QR sits in the same bottom-right corner everywhere it appears, so people learn where to look.

One QR per screen. Version 1 had a "Scan to play" QR on the Pick 'Em board. Two QR codes on one screen makes people hesitate, so the "Get the app" QR replaces it. That QR already does the right thing: phones go to the app store, with a "No thanks, play in the browser" link for anyone who doesn't want the app. Squares and Bracket are scoreboards of their own, so I left the ticker off them.
