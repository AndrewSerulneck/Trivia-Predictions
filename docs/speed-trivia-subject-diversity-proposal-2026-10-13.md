## Recommended focused scope

1. Build subject concentration and recent-subject history once from the existing checked corpus, using persisted metadata and conservatively identified primary entities in question/correct-answer content. Do not count distractors. Disambiguate people, groups and works and normalize their aliases; report unidentified legacy cases rather than falsely claiming complete historical tagging.
2. Enforce a cross-run cooldown for primary people/groups/works before paid uniqueness comparisons. Proposed starting value: 30 days. Retain the existing within-run spread. Apply this to generated candidates, not lifetime validity of existing distinct facts or generic answers such as a country or material.
3. Explicitly steer generation away from people already heavily represented in the bank and toward underrepresented subjects, regions, genres and eras. Select/rotate subject and subtopic briefs across runs rather than repeatedly requesting the same few low-count strings. Enforce the defined concentration policy after generation; prompt instructions alone are insufficient. Calibrate any numerical bank threshold from measured subject distribution before fixing it as policy.
4. Preserve the signed fact uniqueness gate, factual soundness checks, $0.50 total operation cap, accepted-question ceiling and safe shortfalls. Reject a concentrated candidate rather than filling a quantity quota. Add tests for repeated subjects across runs, aliases, works tied to the same artist, unrelated facts sharing generic answers, recent pending/deleted records, and unchanged uniqueness regressions. Record subject spread and actual cost/yield on subsequent authorized scheduled runs.

This can use the current corpus load and metadata fields; prefer no new migration or separate AI editorial judge. Expected extra cloud reads/transferred data and AI calls per run: zero; aggregate locally once instead of rereading the bank per candidate. Earlier rejection can save paid comparisons but may reduce nightly yield. No spending increase or new pilot is proposed; exact post-change useful yield remains to be measured.
